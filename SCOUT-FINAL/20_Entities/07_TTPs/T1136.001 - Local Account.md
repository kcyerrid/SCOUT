# T1136.001 – Local Account

## 1. Technique Overview
**Local Account (T1136.001)** is a sub-technique of **Create Account (T1136)** in **MITRE ATT&CK v18**. Adversaries create **local user accounts** on individual systems to establish persistence or escalate privileges. Because local accounts do not rely on centralized identity providers, they may evade detection mechanisms focused on domain or cloud identities.

This technique is commonly observed after initial compromise of administrative credentials or exploitation of privilege escalation vulnerabilities.

## 2. Adversary Objectives
Adversaries create local accounts to:
- Maintain persistent access to compromised hosts
- Regain access after password resets or account lockouts
- Establish backdoor administrative access
- Blend malicious accounts into legitimate local user populations

## 3. Common Abuse Patterns
- Creating new local user accounts via system utilities or APIs
- Adding attacker-created accounts to local administrator groups
- Using benign or service-like account names to avoid suspicion
- Creating accounts with passwords that evade policy enforcement
- Disabling password expiration or audit settings on malicious accounts

## 4. Detection Considerations
Detection relies on **host-level account management telemetry**, including:
- Monitoring local account creation events
- Alerting on additions to local administrator groups
- Reviewing system logs for account management activity
- Correlating new accounts with anomalous login behavior
- Auditing endpoints for unauthorized local users

## 5. Defensive Mitigations
- Restrict privileges required to create local accounts
- Monitor and alert on local administrator group changes
- Enforce strong password and account lifecycle policies
- Regularly audit local user and group memberships
- Remove unauthorized accounts immediately during response

## 6. Operational Impact
If successful, T1136.001 can:
- Provide durable, host-specific persistence
- Enable administrative control over compromised systems
- Facilitate lateral movement through credential reuse
- Complicate incident response if accounts are overlooked

## 7. Analyst Notes
Local account persistence is often missed in environments heavily focused on directory or cloud identity monitoring. During investigations, analysts should perform **full local account enumerations** on affected systems, including disabled or hidden accounts, to ensure no attacker-created identities remain.
