# T1098.005 – Device Registration

## 1. Technique Overview
**Device Registration (T1098.005)** is a sub-technique of **Account Manipulation (T1098)** in which adversaries **register attacker-controlled devices** to victim accounts within identity, MFA, or device management systems. By enrolling a trusted device, adversaries can achieve **persistent access** that survives password resets and credential rotation.

This technique is particularly relevant in **cloud and hybrid identity environments**, such as Azure AD (Entra ID), Intune, and third-party identity providers.

## 2. Adversary Objectives
Adversaries use device registration to:
- Establish **durable persistence** tied to trusted devices
- Bypass conditional access and MFA controls
- Maintain access despite credential remediation
- Enable long-term operations using legitimate authentication flows

## 3. Common Abuse Patterns
- Registering attacker-owned devices in Azure AD / Entra ID
- Enrolling rogue devices into MDM or endpoint compliance frameworks
- Associating devices with MFA trust relationships
- Leveraging device trust to satisfy conditional access policies
- Using registered devices to mint or refresh authentication tokens

## 4. Detection Considerations
Detection relies on **identity, device, and audit telemetry**, including:
- New device registration events tied to existing user accounts
- Device enrollments occurring outside normal onboarding workflows
- Device registrations from anomalous IP addresses or geolocations
- Sudden trust of devices without corresponding asset inventory records
- Authentication events leveraging newly registered devices

## 5. Defensive Mitigations
- Restrict who can register or join devices to identity platforms
- Require MFA and approval workflows for device registration
- Monitor and alert on new device enrollments
- Enforce device compliance and hardware attestation where supported
- Regularly audit registered devices and remove unknown entries

## 6. Operational Impact
If successful, T1098.005 can:
- Provide **long-term, low-noise persistence**
- Undermine identity-based security controls
- Allow adversaries to bypass MFA and conditional access
- Complicate incident response if device trust is not reviewed

## 7. Analyst Notes
Device registration abuse is a **modern identity persistence technique** that often goes undetected because defenders focus on users and credentials rather than devices. Effective remediation requires enumerating and validating **all registered devices**, not just resetting passwords or revoking tokens.

This technique is increasingly observed in cloud-focused intrusions and should be treated as a high-risk persistence mechanism.
