# T1098.003 – Additional Cloud Roles

## 1. Technique Overview
**Additional Cloud Roles (T1098.003)** is a MITRE ATT&CK sub-technique in which adversaries **assign new or additional roles** to existing cloud identities. By modifying role assignments rather than creating new accounts, attackers gain expanded permissions while blending into legitimate identity and access management activity.

This technique is frequently observed in cloud intrusions following credential compromise or abuse of delegated administrative permissions.

## 2. Adversary Objectives
Adversaries use this technique to:
- Escalate privileges within cloud environments
- Establish durable persistence through trusted identities
- Enable access to sensitive resources or management APIs
- Support follow-on actions such as data exfiltration or infrastructure manipulation

## 3. Common Abuse Patterns
- Assigning high-privilege IAM roles (e.g., Administrator, Owner)
- Granting additional roles to service accounts
- Expanding permissions of compromised cloud users
- Assigning roles that allow credential creation or policy modification
- Adding roles that enable cross-account or cross-project access

## 4. Detection Considerations
Detection depends on **cloud IAM and audit logging**, including:
- Monitoring role assignment and role binding events
- Alerts for assignment of privileged roles
- Detection of role changes outside approved workflows
- Correlating role changes with anomalous login behavior
- Identifying sudden privilege expansion without corresponding business justification

## 5. Defensive Mitigations
- Enforce least privilege for all cloud identities
- Require approval and change management for role assignments
- Monitor and audit IAM role changes continuously
- Limit who can assign or modify roles
- Use just-in-time or time-bound role assignments where supported

## 6. Operational Impact
Successful abuse of T1098.003 can:
- Provide attackers with broad administrative control
- Undermine cloud security boundaries
- Enable rapid escalation and lateral movement across cloud resources
- Complicate incident response by obscuring malicious access within legitimate identities

## 7. Analyst Notes
Cloud role assignment abuse is often overlooked during incident response because defenders focus on credentials rather than **authorization state**. Complete remediation requires reviewing historical and current role bindings to ensure no unauthorized privilege expansion remains.
