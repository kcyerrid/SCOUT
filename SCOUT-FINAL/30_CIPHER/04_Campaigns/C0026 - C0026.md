# C0026 (C0026)

## 1. Executive Summary
**C0026** describes a short, targeted campaign involving the re-registration of expired **ANDROMEDA** C2 domains to identify prior victims in Ukraine and selectively deploy **KOPILUWAK** and **QUIETCANARY**. Reporting and ATT&CK describe activity as consistent with **[[30_CIPHER/03_Threat_Actors/G0010 - Turla|G0010 - Turla]]** operations.

## 2. Attribution & Victimology
- **Attributed actor (confidence: medium):** [[30_CIPHER/03_Threat_Actors/G0010 - Turla|G0010 - Turla]]
- **Victim profile:** Prior ANDROMEDA victims in Ukraine
- **Goal:** Profiling → selective second-stage deployment → data collection/exfil

## 7. Detection & Hunting Ideas
- Monitor for sudden re-contact to historically sinkholed/expired domains linked to prior bot infections.
- Hunt for archive staging (RAR/ZIP creation), 3MB chunking behaviors, and selective file targeting.
- Correlate netstat/arp/net usage with subsequent payload fetch and outbound egress.
