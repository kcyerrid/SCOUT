# C0033 (C0033)

## 1. Executive Summary
**C0033** is a **PROMETHIUM** mobile campaign using **[[30_CIPHER/05_Malware/S0491 - StrongPity|StrongPity (S0491)]]** to target **Android users**, representing the first publicly documented mobile campaign attributed to PROMETHIUM. The operation leverages **waterhole distribution**, **encrypted C2**, and broad **mobile data collection** (notifications, contacts, SMS, call logs).

## 2. Campaign Overview
- **Timeframe:** 2016-05 to 2023-01  
- **Distribution:** compromised website used to distribute trojanized legitimate apps.  
- **Primary intent:** mobile surveillance/espionage.

## 7. Defensive Considerations
1. **Mobile app integrity & vetting:** discourage sideloading; validate app signatures and distribution sources.
2. **Web filtering:** block known waterhole domains and suspicious download chains.
3. **Mobile EDR signals:** alert on excessive notification access, SMS/contacts harvesting, and suspicious broadcast receiver persistence.
4. **Network monitoring:** identify anomalous HTTPS beaconing and data exfil patterns from mobile fleets.
