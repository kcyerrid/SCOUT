# C0032 (C0032)

## 1. Executive Summary
**C0032** is described by MITRE as an **extended campaign** suspected to involve the **TRITON-associated adversaries**, focused on **gaining and maintaining footholds in IT environments**, and explicitly distinct from the **2017 SIS-focused TRITON incident**. The activity highlights **registry-based persistence**, **credential dumping**, and **protocol tunneling** to support sustained access.

## 2. Campaign Overview
- **Timeframe:** 2014-10 to 2017-01 (per MITRE campaign entry)
- **Core behaviors:** VPN persistence, tool staging, timestomping, and web shell placement on Exchange.

## 7. Defensive Considerations
1. **Registry persistence monitoring:** alert on suspicious IFEO keys and debugger values in sensitive hives.
2. **Credential access detections:** monitor LSASS access patterns, credential tool artifacts, and suspicious process ancestry.
3. **Tunneling & remote access:** detect SSH tunneling, abnormal port-protocol mismatches, and unusual RDP enablement behaviors.
4. **Exchange/web shell hunting:** inspect anomalous web content and suspicious IIS/Exchange access patterns.
