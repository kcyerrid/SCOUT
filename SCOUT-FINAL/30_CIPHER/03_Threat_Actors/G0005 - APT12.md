# APT12

## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT12 (MITRE ATT&CK **G0005**) is a China-attributed cyber espionage threat group associated with the names IXESHE, DynCalc, Numbered Panda, and DNSCALC. MITRE ATT&CK and supporting public reporting describe targeting that includes media organizations, high-tech companies, and multiple governments, with campaigns noted against organizations in Japan and Taiwan. Public reporting highlights spearphishing-led intrusions, user-driven execution, exploitation of client applications, and web-service-based command and control (including WordPress/blog infrastructure).

## 2. Attribution Notes
- MITRE ATT&CK attributes APT12 to China and documents multiple overlapping community labels (IXESHE, DynCalc, Numbered Panda, DNSCALC).
- Attribution confidence is constrained by common ecosystem overlap and vendor naming differences; cluster assignment should be grounded in convergent evidence (TTPs + software + victimology), not naming alone.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- **Motivation:** Strategic intelligence collection consistent with state-aligned espionage.
- **Objectives:** Collection of sensitive information from government and high-value private sector targets, and maintenance of access for sustained collection.

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Sectors (reported):** Government entities, high-tech companies, and media outlets.
- **Regions (reported):** Japan and Taiwan are highlighted in major public reporting; broader “multiple governments” targeting is also described.

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- **Delivery & execution:** Spearphishing with malicious document attachments and user-driven execution consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1204.002 - User Execution: Malicious File]].
- **Client exploitation:** Exploitation of vulnerabilities in client applications (e.g., office/document readers) consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1203 - Exploitation for Client Execution]].
- **C2 behaviors:** Use of [[WordPress]]/blogs for communications consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1102.002 - Web Service: Bidirectional Communication]].
- **Dynamic resolution logic:** Use of DNS calculation behaviors documented by MITRE consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1568.003 - Dynamic Resolution: DNS Calculation]].

## 9. Campaign History
- **2012-05 (reporting):** Public reporting on “IXESHE” describes an APT campaign consistent with the APT12 cluster tracked today in MITRE ATT&CK.
- **2014-09 (reporting):** Major public reporting highlights active campaigns targeting organizations in Japan and Taiwan and details spearphishing and C2 patterns (including web-service infrastructure).
- **Ongoing tracking:** MITRE ATT&CK maintains an active group entry for APT12; no authoritative public “end date” is provided in the core sources used here.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- Maintain strong detection and triage coverage for phishing attachment flows consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]] and follow-on execution consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1204.002 - User Execution: Malicious File]].
- Monitor for web-service-based C2 patterns consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1102.002 - Web Service: Bidirectional Communication]] when correlated with victimology and associated malware.
- Treat evidence of DNS calculation behaviors consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1568.003 - Dynamic Resolution: DNS Calculation]] as a higher-signal analytic feature when combined with other corroborating artifacts.
