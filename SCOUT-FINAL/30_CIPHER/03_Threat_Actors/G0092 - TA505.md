## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
TA505 (G0092) is a financially motivated cybercrime group active since at least **2014**, known for **rapidly rotating malware families**, **high-volume distribution campaigns**, and **ransomware operations** (including Clop-referenced campaigns in ATT&CK).

## 2. Attribution Notes
- **MITRE ATT&CK ID:** G0092
- **Associated names/clusters:** Hive0065, Spandex Tempest, CHIMBORAZO
- **Confidence:** High (longstanding public reporting and ATT&CK mapping).

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- **Motivation:** Financial gain.
- **Objectives:** Large-scale compromise for monetization (payload distribution, credential theft, and ransomware impact).

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Targeting:** Broad/global (campaign-dependent).
- **Victim profile:** Often opportunistic at scale; may include targeting aligned with credential theft and ransomware impact.

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
Defender-relevant behaviors highlighted in ATT&CK include:
- **Infrastructure acquisition** (domain registrations impersonating trusted services).
- **C2 over web protocols** (HTTP).
- **Extensive script execution** (PowerShell, cmd, VBS, JavaScript).
- **Defense impairment** (disable or modify security tooling, including Windows Defender via registry changes).
- **Ingress tool transfer** (downloading additional payloads post-compromise).
- **Document tradecraft** (malicious documents abusing DDE; password-protected docs; packed/obfuscated code).

## 8. Infrastructure Patterns
- Domain infrastructure registered to mimic common cloud/file-sharing services (ATT&CK example includes Dropbox impersonation).
- HTTP-based C2 and flexible multi-stage payloading.

## 9. Campaign History
- Long-running evolution since at least 2014; ATT&CK highlights frequent malware changes and ransomware campaign involvement.

## 10. Known Indicators
- Treat as campaign-specific. High-yield detection pivots:
  - Newly registered lookalike domains + malspam delivery.
  - Office/Acrobat → script interpreter chains (PowerShell/cmd/wscript/mshta depending on campaign).
  - Defender/AV tampering via registry and service controls.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- Email + web controls: domain impersonation detection, attachment sandboxing, URL detonation.
- Endpoint hardening: PowerShell constrained language mode where feasible; script block logging; AMSI coverage.
- Detection engineering: alerts on Defender disable attempts, suspicious registry modifications, and staged payload download patterns.
- Ransomware readiness: immutable backups, rapid isolation playbooks, and encryption behavior analytics.

## 12. Analyst Notes
- TA505’s strength is operational agility: frequent tooling shifts and multiple payload types. Maintain detections on **behaviors** (staging/execution/defense impairment), not IOCs.
