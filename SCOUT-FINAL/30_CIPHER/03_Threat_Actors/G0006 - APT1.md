# APT1

## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT1 (MITRE ATT&CK **G0006**, also known as **Comment Crew**) is a China-attributed cyber espionage threat actor publicly associated with **PLA Unit 61398**. Authoritative public reporting describes large-scale, long-running intrusions across numerous industries, characterized by spearphishing, credential theft (including pass-the-hash), email collection, extensive use of registered/compromised domains, and persistent re-entry over months to years.

## 2. Attribution Notes
- MITRE ATT&CK attributes APT1 to the **2nd Bureau of the PLA GSD 3rd Department**, commonly referenced by its MUCD as **Unit 61398**.
- Mandiant’s 2013 report assesses APT1 as likely government-sponsored and links observed activity to infrastructure traced to networks in **Shanghai**.
- The U.S. Department of Justice publicly indicted five PLA officers from **Unit 61398** in 2014 for cyber espionage-related offenses, reinforcing the public association between Unit 61398-linked activity and major U.S.-focused victim sets.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- **Primary motivation:** Cyber espionage for strategic and commercial advantage (as characterized across public reporting).
- **Objectives:** Acquire sensitive corporate/government information (including IP), maintain durable access, and conduct ongoing collection (not “smash-and-grab” operations).

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Sectors:** Broad multi-industry targeting is documented; U.S. legal actions highlight victims in nuclear power, metals, solar products, and a labor organization.
- **Geography:** Reporting emphasizes U.S. victim organizations while describing operations and infrastructure with global reach.

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- **Resource development & infrastructure:** Heavy reliance on [[Registered Domains]] and [[Compromised Domains]] (including hijacked FQDNs associated with [[Hop Point]] infrastructure).
- **Initial access:** [[Spearphishing Attachment]] and [[Spearphishing Link]] patterns consistent with targeted email operations.
- **Credential access & lateral movement enablement:** Use of credential-dumping and credential-reuse techniques, including behaviors consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1003.001 - OS Credential Dumping: LSASS Memory]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1550.002 - Use Alternate Authentication Material: Pass the Hash]].
- **Collection at scale:** Automated discovery/collection scripts and systematic harvesting of files and email (both local and server-side).
- **Operational security & evasion:** Masquerading via legitimate-looking process names consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1036.005 - Masquerading: Match Legitimate Resource Name or Location]].

## 8. Infrastructure Patterns
- Extensive management of [[Registered Domains]] for operations and command-and-control.
- Use of [[Compromised Domains]] (including hijacked FQDNs) associated with [[Hop Point]] infrastructure.
- Distributed [[Command and Control Server]] footprint supporting sustained access.
- Origin attribution narratives frequently reference [[Shanghai]] / [[Pudong New Area]] as an assessed operational locus in major public reporting.

## 9. Campaign History
- **2006–2013 (reported):** Major industry reporting characterizes APT1 as active since at least 2006, with numerous victim intrusions across years culminating in public exposure in early 2013.
- **2013-02 (reporting):** Publication of a detailed public report outlining APT1’s infrastructure, victimology, and tradecraft, including large-scale data theft and repeat access patterns.
- **2014-05 (legal action):** U.S. federal indictment of five PLA Unit 61398 officers for cyber espionage offenses targeting U.S. entities, often cited as a landmark state-actor charging event.

- Incorporate domain-focused threat hunting and brand/domain monitoring aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1583.001 - Acquire Infrastructure: Domains]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1584.001 - Compromise Infrastructure: Domains]].
- Treat combined signals of automated host discovery, staged collection, and archival behaviors (e.g., [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1119 - Automated Collection]] + [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1560.001 - Archive Collected Data: Archive via Utility]]) as higher-confidence indicators of enterprise-scale collection activity.

## 12. Analyst Notes
- **Attribution confidence:** High (multi-source convergence: MITRE ATT&CK attribution + detailed vendor reporting + U.S. legal action referencing Unit 61398).
- **Boundary caution:** “APT1/Comment Crew” is a historical cluster label; modern activity may be tracked under different names or subdivided clusters. Use convergence on TTP + software + infra + victimology rather than name-only mapping.
