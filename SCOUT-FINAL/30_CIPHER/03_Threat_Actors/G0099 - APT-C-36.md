## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT-C-36 (Blind Eagle) is a suspected South America espionage group targeting Colombian government and strategic industries. Detection should prioritize **spearphishing attachment chains**, **script execution (VBScript)**, **scheduled task masquerading**, and **C2 over non-standard ports**.

## 2. Attribution Notes
- ATT&CK characterizes APT-C-36 as a suspected South America espionage group active since at least 2018, with primary targeting in Colombia.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- Espionage-driven collection against government and critical commercial targets.
- Operates via document-led execution and staged downloads of additional tooling.

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Region:** Colombia
- **Sectors:** government, financial, petroleum, manufacturing.

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- Password-protected attachments/archives to evade email gateway controls.
- Embedded scripting (VBScript) triggered on document open and/or macro enablement.
- Establishes persistence using scheduled tasks disguised as legitimate tasks.

## 8. Infrastructure Patterns
- Document delivery + staged retrieval from attacker-controlled domains.
- Non-standard port C2 (documented example includes port 4050).
- Persistence via scheduled tasks that imitate legitimate vendor naming conventions.

## 9. Campaign History
- Public reporting documents sustained spearphishing activity and RAT deployment against Colombian targets since at least 2018.

## 10. Known Indicators
- Capture and retain: original lure documents, archive passwords (if obtained), download URLs, C2 endpoints/ports, scheduled task names/paths, and payload hashes.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
1. **Email gateway hardening:** flag password-protected archives from external senders; enforce detonation with password extraction workflows where permitted.
2. **Script lineage detections:** Office → wscript/cscript chains; monitor child process creation from Office apps.
3. **Scheduled task monitoring:** alert on new tasks with vendor-like naming + suspicious command lines or unusual paths.
4. **Network analytics:** identify egress to rare destinations on non-standard ports; baseline per-segment.
5. **Obfuscation indicators:** ConfuserEx-like packed binaries, anomalous entropy, and runtime unpack behaviors.

## 12. Analyst Notes
- Task masquerading plus non-standard port egress is a strong correlation pair for triage prioritization.
