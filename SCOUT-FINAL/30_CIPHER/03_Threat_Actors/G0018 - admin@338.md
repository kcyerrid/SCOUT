# admin@338

## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
admin@338 (MITRE ATT&CK **G0018**) is a China-based cyber threat group associated with espionage-focused activity. MITRE describes the group as using newsworthy events as lures and primarily targeting organizations involved in financial, economic, and trade policy, often relying on publicly available RATs (e.g., [[30_CIPHER/05_Malware/PoisonIvy]]) alongside other backdoors. Public reporting also links activity described as admin@338 to spearphishing campaigns against Hong Kong media organizations and to broader China-attributed intelligence collection targeting.

## 2. Attribution Notes
- MITRE ATT&CK explicitly characterizes admin@338 as **China-based** but does not provide a public judicial attribution to a specific state organ. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- A FireEye Threat Intelligence report (republished via Google Cloud) notes that other researchers refer to the activity as “admin@338” in the context of Hong Kong media targeting and describes [[Dropbox]] being leveraged for malware communications in that campaign. https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/china-based-threat
- Public cyber-operations tracking resources describe admin@338 as China state-sponsored/suspected and document victim categories (civil society, private sector, government), but these are secondary context sources rather than primary technical attributions. https://www.cfr.org/cyber-operations/2013/10/31/admin338/

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- **Motivations:** Espionage; collection of political and economic intelligence.
- **Objectives:** Obtain sensitive information related to financial/economic/trade policy and influence-relevant media environments; maintain access via commodity RATs and additional backdoors; support sustained collection through iterative targeting.

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Primary targeting (MITRE):** Organizations involved in **financial, economic, and trade policy**. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- **Reported campaign targeting:** Hong Kong media organizations (spearphishing campaign described in public reporting). https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/china-based-threat
- **Reported victim categories:** Civil society, private sector, and government targets have been described in public cyber-operations tracking. https://www.cfr.org/cyber-operations/2013/10/31/admin338/

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- **Delivery:** Spearphishing with malicious Office attachments consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1204.002 - User Execution: Malicious File]]. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- **Exploitation-assisted execution:** Documented use of client-side exploitation for execution (e.g., Microsoft Word vulnerability exploitation) consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1203 - Exploitation for Client Execution]]. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- **Discovery & situational awareness:** Post-compromise use of built-in Windows utilities (account, network, system, file/service discovery) consistent with techniques such as [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1087.001 - Account Discovery: Local Account]], [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1016 - System Network Configuration Discovery]], and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1049 - System Network Connections Discovery]]. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- **Concealment:** Renaming/placement behaviors consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1036.005 - Masquerading: Match Legitimate Resource Name or Location]]. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- **C2 & comms (reported):** Use of web protocols and third-party services (notably [[Dropbox]] in one reported campaign) to support malware communications, aligning conceptually with [[Web Protocols C2]] and “living within” common services. https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/china-based-threat

## 8. Infrastructure Patterns
- [[Dropbox]] used as a communications channel in at least one publicly described spearphishing campaign attributed in part to activity others refer to as admin@338. https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/china-based-threat
- [[Newsworthy Event Lures]] and topical decoy themes used to increase open/click rates for spearphishing delivery. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/
- Predominant reliance on [[Malicious Microsoft Office Documents]] as delivery artifacts and on common web protocol communications patterns for C2. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/

## 9. Campaign History
- **2013-10-31:** Public cyber-operations tracking publishes an entry describing admin@338 targeting (civil society/private sector/government), with suspected China state sponsorship. https://www.cfr.org/cyber-operations/2013/10/31/admin338/
- **2015-08 (reported):** FireEye describes a spearphishing campaign targeting Hong Kong media organizations where other researchers refer to the activity as admin@338 and notes [[Dropbox]] used for malware communications. https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/threat-intelligence/china-based-threat
- **Ongoing (undated, MITRE):** MITRE summarizes admin@338 as an active cluster targeting financial/economic/trade policy organizations using spearphishing attachments and client exploitation, supported by post-compromise discovery via native tools. https://attack.mitre.org/groups/G0018/

## 10. Known Indicators
No stable public indicators are included in this note. Consult the primary reporting for case-specific IOC context and validate within your own telemetry.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- Reduce spearphishing risk and attachment execution exposure aligned with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1204.002 - User Execution: Malicious File]] through layered email controls and endpoint protections.
- Maintain strong patch management and exploit mitigations for document-handling and client software paths aligned with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1203 - Exploitation for Client Execution]].
- Increase detection for discovery patterns aligned with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1082 - System Information Discovery]], [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1016 - System Network Configuration Discovery]], and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1049 - System Network Connections Discovery]] when correlated with suspicious document delivery and unusual outbound communications.
- Monitor for abuse of common cloud services (e.g., [[Dropbox]]) as potential covert comms channels, using behavior correlation rather than IP reputation alone.

## 12. Analyst Notes
- admin@338 is a compactly documented cluster in MITRE with a small set of well-cited techniques and software; avoid over-expanding TTPs beyond what sources support.
- “China-based” is a strong descriptor in MITRE, but sponsor/organizational attribution remains less explicit in public primary sources; treat sponsor claims conservatively.
- The actor name is often used in relation to a specific Hong Kong media campaign; ensure your internal entity resolution distinguishes between “cluster” and “campaign” when ingesting third-party reports.
