## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT19 (G0073) is an espionage-oriented threat actor documented in ATT&CK with a playbook that combines **user-driven initial access** (drive-by and malicious attachments) and **high-signal Windows tradecraft**: persistence via services, DLL-based execution flow hijacking, and **LOLBins/proxy execution** via regsvr32 and rundll32. ATT&CK also maps APT19 to the use of Cobalt Strike.

## 2. Attribution Notes
ATT&CK provides a group entity and technique mapping but does not, in the material captured here, assert sponsor attribution fields suitable for locking into YAML. Treat sponsor/country attribution as unknown unless explicitly supported by ATT&CK or primary research sources.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- Espionage-driven access and collection
- Establish persistence and enable repeatable execution
- Conduct discovery (system/network/user) to support operations and operator movement

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- **Execution:** PowerShell aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1059.001 - Command and Scripting Interpreter: PowerShell]].
- **Persistence:** Windows services aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1543.003 - Create or Modify System Process: Windows Service]].
- **Defense evasion / stealth:** hidden PowerShell windows aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1564.003 - Hide Artifacts: Hidden Window]] and encoded/obfuscated payloads aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1027.010 - Obfuscated Files or Information: Command Obfuscation]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1027.013 - Obfuscated Files or Information: Encrypted/Encoded File]].
- **Proxy execution (LOLBins):** regsvr32/rundll32 aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1218.010 - System Binary Proxy Execution: Regsvr32]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1218.011 - System Binary Proxy Execution: Rundll32]].

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- Flag regsvr32/rundll32 executions with unusual network or child-process behavior; correlate with script-block logging and image-load telemetry.
- Hunt for suspicious service creation paired with encoded PowerShell and registry modifications.
- Detect drive-by compromise outcomes through browser/process telemetry and follow-on LOLBin execution sequences.
