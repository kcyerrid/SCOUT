## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT39 (G0087) is an Iranian cyber-espionage activity set attributed in ATT&CK to the Ministry of Intelligence and Security (MOIS) via Rana Intelligence Computing, operating since at least 2014. ATT&CK documents targeting emphasis on travel/hospitality, academia, and telecommunications, with tradecraft spanning spearphishing, public-facing exploitation, credential theft, data staging, and exfiltration over C2.

## 2. Attribution Notes
- ATT&CK frames APT39 as MOIS-linked activity through a front company (Rana Intelligence Computing).
- Treat attribution as **high confidence** only to the extent explicitly stated by ATT&CK and directly cited reporting; avoid extrapolation beyond those sources.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- **Primary:** espionage and surveillance (monitoring/tracking individuals and organizations of interest)
- **Operational objectives:** credential access, internal reconnaissance, file collection, local staging, and exfiltration via C2

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Sectors:** travel/hospitality, academic, telecommunications
- **Geography:** Iran and international targeting across Asia, Africa, Europe, and North America (per ATT&CK summary)

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- **Initial access:** [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]] / [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.002 - Phishing: Spearphishing Link]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1190 - Exploit Public-Facing Application]].
- **Execution & tooling:** multi-language scripting (PowerShell/VBS/Python/AutoIt) via [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1059 - Command and Scripting Interpreter]] and subtechniques.
- **Credential access:** [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1003 - OS Credential Dumping]] (including [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1003.001 - OS Credential Dumping: LSASS Memory]]) and password store access [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1555 - Credentials from Password Stores]].

## 8. Infrastructure Patterns

- **Staging paths:** local aggregation directories and archive utilities prior to exfiltration.
- **Masquerade:** filename/location mimicry aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1036.005 - Masquerading: Match Legitimate Resource Name or Location]].

## 9. Campaign History
- **At least 2014–present (reported):** long-running espionage and personal-information-focused theft (see linked reporting in References).

## 10. Known Indicators
No stable, long-lived indicators included in ATT&CK. Prefer incident-specific IOCs (domains, host artifacts, tool hashes) from your telemetry and validated reporting.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- **High-signal detections:** LSASS access/dumps; password store access; unusual archive utilities on endpoints; BITS job creation; persistence via Startup folder/LNK modification.
- **Telemetry priorities:** process creation (with command line), PowerShell logging, Sysmon (ProcessCreate, ImageLoad, FileCreate, Registry), DNS logs, proxy/HTTP logs, and authentication telemetry for brute force/valid accounts.
- **Hardening:** phishing-resistant MFA for remote access, reduce exposed surface of public-facing apps, least privilege, and constrain admin tooling.
