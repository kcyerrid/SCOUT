## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT33 (G0064) is a suspected Iran-linked threat actor active since at least 2013, with public reporting describing multi-sector targeting that includes aviation and energy. ATT&CK-tracked operations reflect a blend of spearphishing-led access, password spraying, web-protocol C2, and use of both commodity tools and bespoke malware families.

## 2. Attribution Notes
MITRE ATT&CK frames APT33 as “suspected Iranian” and lists associated tracking names HOLMIUM, Elfin, and Peach Sandstorm. Public reporting varies in the level of confidence and sponsor specifics; this note uses the conservative, ATT&CK-aligned posture.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- Strategic espionage and information theft
- Credential access to enable durable access and lateral movement
- Potential disruption/destruction in some reported activity (varies by campaign and source)

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Regions (reported):** United States, Saudi Arabia, South Korea
- **Sectors (reported):** aviation and energy prominently, plus broader enterprise verticals

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- Initial access via **[[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]]** and **[[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.002 - Phishing: Spearphishing Link]]**, including lures designed to drive **user execution**.
- Credential-focused tradecraft including **[[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1110.003 - Brute Force: Password Spraying]]** and harvesting from insecure storage locations.
- Post-compromise use of **PowerShell** and common post-exploitation frameworks, paired with malware families linked in ATT&CK.
- Reported integration of cloud identity compromise workflows, including use of compromised Office 365 accounts.

## 10. Known Indicators
No public, stable indicators are included in this note.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- Enforce phishing-resistant MFA and conditional access; prioritize monitoring for anomalous cloud sign-ins and token/session behavior.
- Tune detections for password spraying and credential access anomalies; lock down legacy auth where possible.
- Increase telemetry for PowerShell and scheduled task creation; investigate abnormal Office-parented process trees.
- Segment high-value networks and restrict lateral movement paths; apply least privilege and strong credential hygiene.

## 12. Analyst Notes
**Confidence:** Medium-high. High-level characterization and multiple technique/tool associations are grounded in ATT&CK and reputable reporting, but campaign-to-campaign variability is material.
