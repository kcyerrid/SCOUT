## 1. BLUF / Executive Summary
APT37 (G0067) is a North Korean state-sponsored espionage actor active since at least 2012. ATT&CK describes primary targeting in South Korea with a broader regional footprint, and documents tradecraft spanning spearphishing, persistence via autoruns, credential harvesting, and cloud/web-service-enabled command-and-control.

## 2. Attribution Notes
ATT&CK attributes APT37 to North Korea and notes significant overlap in how North Korean cyber activity is clustered across the industry (some sources aggregate multiple clusters under broader umbrellas). This note retains ATT&CK’s framing and treats aliases as tracking-name variants unless otherwise specified.

## 3. Motivations & Objectives
- Strategic intelligence collection (espionage)
- Credential theft to expand and sustain access
- Endpoint surveillance/collection (including local data and multimedia capture capabilities in reported tooling)

## 4. Targeting Profile
- **Primary:** South Korea (reported)
- **Additional regions (reported):** Japan, Vietnam, Russia, Nepal, China, India, Romania, Kuwait, and parts of the Middle East
- **Victim themes:** government-adjacent, policy, and regional security interests

## 5. Tradecraft Overview
- Spearphishing-driven initial access and user execution aligned to [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1566.001 - Phishing: Spearphishing Attachment]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1204.002 - User Execution: Malicious File]].
- Post-compromise web/CSP blending via [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1071.001 - Application Layer Protocol: Web Protocols]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1102.002 - Web Service: Bidirectional Communication]].
- Host persistence via autoruns consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1547.001 - Boot or Logon Autostart Execution: Registry Run Keys / Startup Folder]].
- Collection and credential theft reflected in [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1555.003 - Credentials from Password Stores: Credentials from Web Browsers]] and [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1005 - Data from Local System]].
- Execution via multiple interpreters consistent with [[20_Entities/07_TTPs/T1059 - Command and Scripting Interpreter]] and relevant sub-techniques.

## 10. Known Indicators
No public, stable indicators are included in this note.

## 11. Defensive Recommendations
- Prioritize protection of high-risk mailboxes and endpoints; emphasize anti-phishing controls and anomaly detection for user-driven execution.
- Increase visibility into cloud/web-service traffic used for C2-like behavior; focus on deviations from organizational baselines.
- Strengthen credential hygiene and monitoring for browser credential access patterns and suspicious persistence via autoruns.

## 12. Analyst Notes
**Confidence:** High for attribution and broad tradecraft (ATT&CK). Incident-level clustering can vary across vendors due to North Korea cluster overlap; preserve case context when correlating.
