# Installing the Obsidian Community Plug-ins

1\. Start by clicking on the Vault Settings (gear icon).

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/dca5b927-ebe4-484e-afae-446ad25cbd6c/ascreenshot_497141eb9b4f4cb1ac7049349eb9a29c_text_export.jpeg)


2\. Navigate to Community Plug-ins on the left-hand side of the Vault Settings dialog.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/a86ff54d-3fdf-4222-9316-191a77d80ce9/ascreenshot_4d40749cf1684ce7a845770f52046200_text_export.jpeg)


3\. Click the button to enable community plug-ins

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/285b7ffa-9ad6-4be0-b082-e1f12b5417d4/ascreenshot_5702325361444206b0f194c0ba5c6865_text_export.jpeg)


4\. Click the Browse button in the Community plug-ins section.

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/94d27e33-b8b4-40f7-b8c7-60be81b91fa1/ascreenshot_4c9e29e2e0e44ce6a3aae447e9958d09_text_export.jpeg)


5\. Begin typing the word `templater`

![](https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2026-02-09/15ad0dc3-895c-4a73-afb6-54d9f401be5b/ascreenshot_400d6a2dac4f4372b7bc325fd5d5d9af_text_export.jpeg)
